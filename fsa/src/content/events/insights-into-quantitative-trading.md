An evening with quantitative traders and researchers from firms recruiting out
of Melbourne. Expect a candid panel rather than a pitch: what a research cycle
looks like, which parts of your degree carry over, and how the interview
process is structured from first-round test to final superday.

Open to all year levels. Members are seated first; general admission opens a
week before the event.
