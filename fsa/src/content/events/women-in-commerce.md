Now in its fourth year, Women in Commerce pairs a frank panel discussion with a
mentoring hour. Panellists cover the parts of a finance career that rarely make
it into a graduate brochure: negotiating a first offer, finding sponsorship
inside a firm, and navigating a floor where you may be the only woman on it.

All students are welcome to attend.
