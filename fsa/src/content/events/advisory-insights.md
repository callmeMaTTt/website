Advisory sits between the classroom and the transaction, and it is one of the
harder graduate paths to picture from the outside. This evening brings together
associates and managers across corporate finance, restructuring and transaction
services to talk through live deal work.

The second half is structured networking in small rotating groups, so everyone
gets time with the firms rather than a scramble at the bar.
