I grew up travelling. Korea, Taiwan, China and Canada with my Dad, Europe, USA, Vietnam with my Mum. I loved it. But I was always craving a particular kind of trip, one where there was no planning, no list of places to visit and no return date.

So at 18, I booked a one-way flight to Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. 

Here are some of the things I've learnt on my travels.

### travel for the people
I always used to be quite shy and resesrved. I was never really one to initiate a conversation when I was with strangers. So you could imagine how I felt being in a country where I knew no one and didn't even speak the primary language. During this trip, I had to learn how to put myself out there and so I made it a challenge to say hi to everyone I saw in the hostel. This is honestly the method. I met so many great people and had so many meals with people I never would've met, if I didn't just say hi. My most memorable experiences from travelling have always been the random interactions I've had people and so I big part of why I travel now, is for the people. 

### learn through experience not school
